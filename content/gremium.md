## How to Edit This Page

This page is easily editable in markdown! To update member information:

1. **To add a photo:** Replace the `<div class="photo-placeholder">` with an image tag:
   ```html
   <img src="/images/your-photo.jpg" alt="Name">
   ```

2. **To change names:** Simply edit the text in `<h4 class="gremium-name">...</h4>`

3. **To update titles:** Edit the text in `<h3 class="gremium-title">...</h3>`

4. **To change descriptions:** Update the paragraph in `<p class="gremium-description">...</p>`

5. **To add new members:** Copy an entire `<div class="gremium-member">...</div>` block and paste it, then edit the content!

### Image Setup

Place your images in the `static/images/` folder, and they'll be accessible at `/images/filename.jpg` in your markdown.
